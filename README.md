# YTMstats

> ⚠️ **Note:** YTMstats is an unofficial project and is not affiliated with, authorized by, or endorsed by Google, YouTube, or YouTube Music.

## 🔎 What is YTMstats?

YTMstats is a fork of Pear Desktop with additional features focused on tracking and visualizing your YouTube Music listening history.

The main additions are a comprehensive **listening statistics system**, personal and global play counts, and fixes to the desktop interface.

## 📊 Listening Statistics

YTMstats automatically tracks your listening history and provides statistics across different time periods.

- 🎵 **Top Songs**
- 🎤 **Top Artists**
- 💿 **Top Albums**
- 📅 Last 24 hours
- 📅 Last 7 days
- 📅 Last 30 days
- 📅 Last 365 days
- ♾️ All-time statistics

Statistics include play counts and listening time, allowing you to see what you've been listening to over different periods.

## 🎧 Personal & Global Play Counts

Play counts are displayed throughout the application.

- 🎧 **Personal play count** — how many times you've played a song
- 🌐 **Global play count** — the song's YouTube Music play count

These counts are shown directly alongside songs in areas such as playlists, search results, queues and your library.
